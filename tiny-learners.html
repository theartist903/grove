About Room

Our Tiny Learners room caters for our smallest friends from birth to 18 months of age. The room has a homely feel with a variety of toys to stimulate and entertain your child whatever their age. We understand how hard it can be to leave your baby so we will do all that we can in order to make the transition for you and your child as easy as possible. We offer a course of settling in sessions where you and your child can get to know the room and its staff, to help us get to know more about your child and their routine. The staff in the Tiny Learners will follow your baby’s home routine as closely as possible.

Baby Feeds

We ask that parents provide all babies, up to approximately one year, with their own formula milks and weaning foods. Once your child is ready to move on, we will provide fresh milk and solid foods

Nappies

Parents are asked to provide their own nappies and creams. Laugh n Learn will provide wipes.

Clothing

Parents are requested to provide spare sets of clothing

Comforters

We appreciate that some children require a comforter or special toy to help them settle, they are very welcome to bring them, but please make sure they are clearly labelled

Come Visit Us

Laugh n Learn invite you to come and visit us. We are open from Monday to Friday. If you would like to see the setting at either branch do not hesitate to call and book a visit for you and your child.
