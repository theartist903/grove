About Room

Our wrap around club provides before and after school care for children between 5 and 12 years. We offer a pick up and drop off service (subject to availability) Please enquire for more details

During half term breaks and summer holidays we are able to offer half and full day session.
We offer a variety of activities to suit each child’s needs.

9.30 – 4.30: £20
Half day: £15
Laugh ‘n’ Learn has been established to provide care for children aged 5 – 12. Laugh ‘n’ Learn gives priority to siblings of registered children. Laugh ‘n’ Learn Club normally follows the BCC term dates; please contact us for details. The Holiday Club will run from 7.30am-6.00pm. We have spacious rooms supervised by dedicated play leaders. Children will share mealtimes with the nursery children. Hot lunches and tea are included. Snacks & drinks are provided throughout the day. Children are offered a variety of indoor and outdoor activities to choose from which inlcude, messy play, arts and craft, ICT, sensory play, board games, role play, team games, and water play.

Continued Learning

We are aware that it is very important for children to have a routine and stay involved with their educational needs. We provide educational activities and learning through fun set out tasks.

Actively Involve

Being actively involved is still crucial through the holiday period. We allow children to engage in different activities and learning opportunities to continue their learning for future success.

Holiday Fun

We ensure that holidays are always fun for our children with various different tasks and activities to be involved. Holidays at our setting are always ones to remember!

Outings

To make the holidays even better we take the children on trips such as cinemas and play areas to ensure that outdoor learning is also promoted with real life experiences.

Come Visit Us

Laugh n Learn invite you to come and visit us. We are open from Monday to Friday. If you would like to see the setting at either branch do not hesitate to call and book a visit for you and your child.
