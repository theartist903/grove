Our Policies

Section 1: Health, Hygiene & Safety Protocol
  • Strict Illness & Exclusion Window: To maintain a clean and infection-free environment, children suffering from active sickness, fever, or diarrhea cannot attend the nursery until 48 hours after their last symptom. Parents must immediately notify management of any communicable diseases so we can alert our community.
  • Zero-Tolerance Mobile & Media Policy: To fiercely protect our children from unauthorized photography or digital risk, no personal mobile phones may be used or visible in the presence of children. Staff and parents must step completely outside the children's zones to answer calls.
  • Hygiene Minimization Rules: To limit external contamination, we follow strict sanitation guidelines. Parents are requested not to bring large personal bags, toys, strollers, or outside food items from home into the nursery premises. All baseline items used inside are sterilized daily by our team.
Section 2: Admissions, Security & Conduct Protocol
  • Secure Arrivals & Authorized Departures: Upon arrival, all children must be formally signed in by their guardian. If a child is being collected by someone other than their regular parent, an agreed security password and verified written description must be provided to management in advance. No child will ever be released to an unauthorized individual.
  • Positive Behaviour & Community Safeguarding: We utilize supportive, age-appropriate, positive distraction and praise strategies to guide children’s behaviour. We strictly do not tolerate bullying or abusive behaviour among children, staff, or visitors. Inappropriate language or aggressive actions from adults will result in an immediate bar from the premises.
  • Confidentiality & Data Protection: Parents have complete access to the records and files of their own children, but absolute privacy is maintained for all other registered families. Sensitive child safety information is securely locked and shared exclusively on a strict need-to-know basis.
Section 3: Late Pick-Up Policy (Past 17:30)

At The Grove, we look after our dedicated team of educators just as fiercely as we look after your children. We recognize that traffic delays or unexpected professional duties happen. To keep our nursery safe and well-staffed, we implement the following late collection guidelines:

  • The Grace Window (17:00 – 17:30): While our general half-day shifts wind down earlier, full-day parents have a smooth transition window until 17:30.
  • Late Late-Fee Fee (Post 17:30): If pick-up occurs after our final closing time of 17:30, a late collection fee of PKR 500 per half-hour (or part thereof) will apply. This fee goes directly to compensating the specific staff members who stay overtime to care for your child.
  • Communication is Key: If you anticipate a delay, please contact our front office control desk immediately so we can prepare your child and reassure them.
