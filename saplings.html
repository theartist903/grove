About Room

The Developmental Focus:

Language expansion, active social interaction, and cooperative play.

The Environment:

A dynamic space rich in real-world objects and loose parts. Here, children are encouraged to direct their own play, build friendships, and express their developing personalities through creative storytelling and sensory arts.

Meals

With parents request we will be able to provide nursery food and start encouraging their independent eating. Staff will be able to support children using simple cutlery and crockery. 

Nappies

Parents are asked to provide their own nappies and creams. The Grove will provide wipes.

Physical Activities

At The Grove we encourage our saplings to get physical and explore different environments such as the outdoor area. We will asking  parents to provide spare clothes as we engage them with messy play.

Learning Through Play

We encourage them to explore the world around them to be able to learn and grow. At this age sensory exploratory is crucial to develop their young minds.

Come Visit Us

The Grove invite you to come and visit us. We are open. If you would like to see the setting do not hesitate to call and book a visit for you and your child.
