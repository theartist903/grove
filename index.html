Meals

Freshly prepared lunch/tea meals are provided by our staff ensuring that children have a healthy balanced diet. Meals are carefully planned to be nutritious and varied. Any special dietary requirements will be catered for.

Curriculum

Children learn about the world around them from the moment they are born. The care and education offered by The Grove helps children to continue to do this by providing all children with interesting activities that are right for their age and stage of development.

Behaviour

We encourage high standards of behaviour from all our children. All unacceptable behaviour will be dealt with firmly but fairly. Parents will be informed if unacceptable behaviour is noticed

A Word

From Our Managing Director

“Welcome to The Grove, where playing and learning go hand in hand. 

Our nursery was born from a deeply personal journey. Having lived in the United Kingdom for over 15 years, we experienced first-hand the positive impact that a nurturing, stimulating and thoughtfully designed early years environment can have on a child. When we returned to Pakistan, we wanted our child to enjoy the same sense of security, curiosity and opportunity during these important early years. This experience inspired us to create a nursery that brings together the qualities we valued abroad with the warmth, traditions and cultural values of our own community.

We envisioned a state-of-the-art day nursery where world class standards of early years care meet a genuine understanding of local families and their values. A place where children can learn, explore and flourish in a nurturing environment, while feeling completely at home. Here, your child will experience exceptional care, inspiring learning and a strong sense of belonging.
