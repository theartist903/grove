Meals

Freshly prepared lunch/tea meals are provided by our qualified chef ensuring that children have a healthy balanced diet. Meals are carefully planned to be nutritious and varied. All food served is HMC Halal. Any special dietary requirements will be catered for.

Curriculum

Children learn about the world around them from the moment they are born. The care and education offered by Laugh n Learn helps children to continue to do this by providing all children with interesting activities that are right for their age and stage of development.

Behaviour

We encourage high standards of behaviour from all our children. All unacceptable behaviour will be dealt with firmly but fairly. Parents will be informed if unacceptable behaviour is noticed
