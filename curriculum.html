The Curriculum

Our Combi-Curriculum & Learning

  • At The Grove, we believe children learn about the world around them from the moment they are born. Rather than using rigid checklists, our premium UK-style curriculum combines the artistic freedom of Reggio Emilia with the wonder-led Curiosity Approach to deliver learning through purposeful play.
  • Our daily educational framework targets six core areas of early childhood development, preparing your child for top-tier international primary schooling while honouring local cultural norms.
Personal, Social & Emotional Well-being
  • Building deep self-confidence and valuing personal achievements from an early age.
  • Forming healthy, respectful friendships with peers and educators.
  • Learning self-regulation, mindfulness, and keeping to daily care routines.
  • Developing early independence, including personal hygiene and looking after their environment.
Communication, Language & Literacy
  • Expanding descriptive vocabulary through immersive storytelling and daily conversation circles.
  • Building a strong phonetic foundation by recognizing letter sounds and patterns.
  • Fostering a love for reading using authentic books in cozy learning corners.
  • Encouraging early motor coordination for independent mark-making and writing attempts.
Mathematical & Logical Thinking
  • Developing real-world spatial awareness (understanding sizes, shapes, and distances).
  • Learning numbers naturally by counting authentic loose parts, pebbles, and wooden blocks.
  • Introducing early problem-solving skills through pattern-making and sorting.
Physical Mastery & Coordination
  • Gross Motor Skills: Balancing, climbing, and running safely in our structured indoor and outdoor play spaces.
  • Fine Motor Skills: Controlling small muscles using real-world tools, safety scissors, clay, and sensory textiles.
  • Teaching children about health, nutrition, and bodily awareness.
Understanding Our World
  • Exploring the natural world, changing seasons, and how living things grow.
  • Integrating basic technology and early scientific tools (magnifying glasses, light boxes) safely.
  • Deep Local Roots: Instilling deep respect for our local cultural heritage, family values, and community customs.
